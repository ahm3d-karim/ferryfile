Welcome to Ferryfile

Convert files by dragging them. Try it once with this sample.

Try it

  1. 1 Start dragging the sample Keep holding the mouse button
  2. 2 Press while you drag Works in Explorer and anywhere else you drag files
  3. 3 Drop it on the drawer It slides in from the right edge of the screen

You can also right-click a file in Explorer and pick Convert with Ferryfile.
